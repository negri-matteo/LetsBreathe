let’s breathe

Breathe, hold, improve. Apnea with heart rate from Apple Watch.

Download on the App Store Source code on GitHub

Four tabs, one breath

Breathe, hold, see how it went.

A circle that follows your breath

  • Five ready-made timers and five quick presets.
  • Each phase from 0 to 60 seconds, up to 30 cycles.
  • Sounds, beeps and vibration, each on its own switch.

One tap starts, one stops

  • Your previous attempt is the time to beat.
  • A sound or a vibration when you beat it.
  • Recovery timed between attempts.

Every session, attempt by attempt

  • Best time, heart rate and lowest SpO₂.
  • Charts of your progress over time.
  • CSV export for Excel and Numbers.

Heart rate, from your wrist

  • Your iPhone opens the app on the Watch when you start.
  • Heart rate arrives in real time.
  • Nothing is left in Fitness when the session ends.

Never in the water alone

  1. Safety first. You can black out without warning.
  2. Never alone. Someone nearby who knows what you are doing.
  3. Seated or lying down. Never standing or driving.
  4. No hyperventilating before a hold.

Not a medical device: heart rate and SpO₂ are for personal wellness only.

Everything stays on your iPhone.

No server, no account, no ads. Health data is only read, never changed.
